# CALE/ACEJ 2026 — Toronto, Oct 22–24

**Theme:** Guidance and Accountability Towards a More Just Society
**Host:** Lincoln Alexander School of Law, Toronto Metropolitan University
**Original proposal deadline:** June 30, 2026 — **passed**
**Contacts:** Deanne Sowter, Alexandra Mogyoros

## Why chase this anyway
- Explicitly welcomes junior scholars, graduate students, and those working on
  legal ethics outside the academy — no faculty-appointment gate.
- Accepts works in progress.
- Held at the school where you'll be teaching (JUR 108/404), your first month in
  Toronto.
- TMU Law Review is planning a special issue from conference papers, revised
  drafts due ~2.5–3 months after — a real publication path if a slot opens.
- Small conference — dropouts and late-add slots happen more often than at
  large venues.

## What to send
Email drafted at `cfp/correspondence/cale-acej-inquiry.md` — asks plainly whether
late submissions or a waitlist spot exist, attaches the 200-word abstract
(`cfp/papers/supervision-paradox/abstracts/200w-cale.md`), and offers to send the
full abstract at whatever length they'd take if there's interest.

## Not automated
Sending the email is a judgment call about tone with people you'll likely see in
person soon (you're about to be a colleague at the host school) — better to send
it yourself, possibly after a tweak to the personal framing.
