# 100-word bio

Michael Bryant is the 35th Attorney General of Ontario and founder of Justack
(justack.ai). He previously served as CEO of Legal Aid BC and as General Counsel
and Executive Director of the Canadian Civil Liberties Association (2018–2022), and
is currently Counsel at Fogler Rubinoff LLP. He holds a BA and MA from the
University of British Columbia, a JD from Osgoode Hall Law School, and an LLM
from Harvard Law School. His current research applies empirical and doctrinal
methods to legal-AI regulation, including the double standard between AI-sourced
and human-sourced error in professional discipline, and a quantitative measure of
legislative purpose-effect divergence named for civil-liberties advocate Alan
Borovoy.
